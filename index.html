About Our Business

About Trilokpati Photography

Trilokpati Photography is a photography service business based in Andheri East, Mumbai, Maharashtra. We provide photography service options for personal occasions, celebrations and selected business projects.

Our service categories include wedding photography, pre-wedding photography, event coverage, portrait sessions, family photography and commercial photography. Each project can have different requirements, schedules and deliverables.

We encourage clients to discuss their event date, preferred photography style, location, expected coverage and budget before confirming a booking. Service availability, pricing and deliverables are communicated during the inquiry process.

Our goal is to make the inquiry process clear and convenient so clients can understand the available services before making a decision.
